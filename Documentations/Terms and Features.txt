- The key element of the game.
- Active GameObeject, and LOTS of them!

- Many type of [UNIT] will have different role / propose, stats
  (and special mechanics unique to a type, in the future)

- Primary Features:
  > Movement
    > Destination
      > [BASE]
      ? "Waypoint Flag" (temp. Destination)
    > Pattern (mid-journey behaviour)
    > Speed

  > Crowd physics ([UNIT] will not stacking / overlapping each other and form cluster / formation)
    !!> Simple and light-weight logic to handle crowd interactions

    > 'Weight' (resistance to knockback / crowding condition)
    > 'Repelling force' (for repelling other [UNIT] away)
    > Handle external forces, like "Knockback" from melee combats

  > Basic stats
    > TeamID (ownership / factions)
    > HP
    
  > Melee combat stats
    > Damage
    > 'Knockback Force' (post-combat consequence)
    ? (on-melee-attack trigger)


- Interactions
  A> Collision with an opposing [UNIT]: [Melee Combat]
    Both [UNIT]'s resolve at the same time

    A1> Take damage: deduce HP
        > remove* from game if HP goes down to <= 0

    A2> Take knockback force and apply to [Crowd Physics]
    A3> Mark each other a tiny grace-period to prevent repeating same-pair melee collisions
    

  B> Approaching [BASE]
    B0> Priority: ignore and be exempted from all [UNIT] interactions, only if [BASE] entry occured

    B1> Entering the [BASE] by condition:
      1.1> Enemy [BASE], regardless if it's the intended target
        1.1.1> Transfer data to target [BASE], to be resolved as invader

      1.2> Friendly [BASE], only if it is the assigned destination
        1.2.1> Transfer data to target [BASE], to be resolved as reinforcement

=============================
[BASE] Base
=============================
Synonym: Fort, Camp, Garrison
=============================
- Serve as a source of [UNIT] generation; manpower "Recruitment"
- Dynamically changing elements
  > with [UNIT] as the primary means, [RALLY] to and take over the [BASE]

- Also serve as key elements for game-ending condition
  > territoral control: acquire and gather up manpower, the 'resource' of the game
  > conquest: eliminate enemy's control over the [BASE]s, winning the game
  > defense: prevent enemy from doing the same: losing all controlled [BASE]s and you are defeated!

- Primary Features:
  > [UNIT] Recruitment
    > specific [UNIT] type
    > periodically, in rate of headcount per second

  > [UNIT] storage: [UNIT RESERVE]
    > mini database representing types and numbers of [UNIT] the [BASE] currently holding

  > [UNIT] deployment: forming [ARMY] to [RALLY] to target [BASE]
    > Spawn [UNIT] GameObjects in a pattern / formation, assign and initialize stats and data

  > Data resolution with incoming friendly [UNIT]s
    > [wip] go straight to [UNIT RESERVE] tally
    
  > Combat resolution against [INVADER]
    1> Loop melee combat and sum up result; "fight to the death" style
    2> Iterate through list of [INVADER]s against [BASE]'s [UNIT RESERVE]
    3> [BASE] takeovers will temp disable recruitment and conversion capability


? Planned Features
  ? Taking in injured friendly [UNIT]s need recovery as part of game mechanic limiter / challenge
  ? left-over [UNIT] from the combat within the [BASE] also subject to recovery / partially capable

  ? [UNIT] conversion, from one type to another type
    Required: Recruitment halted without required [UNIT] in the [BASE]'s [RESERVE]
    Optional: Shorten process time -or- as parallel conversion process
    Tactical: Shifting [UNIT] types, adjusting composition to suit the tactical needs



=============================
Melee Combat
=============================
- one melee combat involves two opposing [UNIT]s collided into each other mid-way to their [RALLY] destination
- each [UNIT] attack the other [UNIT] with their melee stats: exactly one time each.
- Then, if survive, get knocked back (almost always).

- Singleton class to manage all the [Melee Combat]s to ensure that:
  > no duplicated resolutions, at least within the same frame / game tick
  > no overkills; no hitting the dead / dying [UNIT]s and suffer its counter-attack for free
  > special cases and logics
    1> [UNIT] will take [BASE] entry as a priority and ignore all other combats in the process



=============================
[UNIT RESERVE] Unit Reserve
=============================
- database to store and represent [UNIT] a certain GameObject has stored within
- Raw data, in a list of [UNIT] types, each has
  > [UNIT STATS] object: to define [UNIT] type and its characteristics 
  > headcount: total number of [UNIT]s something actually has
  > busycount: a way to reserve certain amount of [UNIT] for use with all sort of actions and mechanics
