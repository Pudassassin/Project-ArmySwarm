Project Army Swarm
v0.1

Win-lose condition:
  - When a player has no [BASE] under their [Control], that player is eliminiated from the game.
    * the final chance is that their [UNIT] already [RALLY]ed on the way to a [BASE] manage to take over it and retain the player's [Control]
      **(maybe a technically troublesome exception to allow: gametesting needed)

  - THE GOAL: Elminate all opponents' [Control] by the means of territoral control


Mechanics
  - [RALLY]ing [UNIT]s to battle and take over [BASE]s
    > option to select source(s) [BASE] and a destination [BASE]
    > option to [RALLY] selected [UNIT] type(s)
    > option to specify amount to [RALLY]: in percentage or fixed amount

  - Each [BASE] varies in [UNIT] it recruits, and how fast the recruitment process goes
    > Recruited [UNIT] is stored inside its [UNIT CONTAINER]
    > Can accept [UNIT]s sent its way from other friendly [BASE](s)
    > Use [UNIT] in [UNIT CONTAINER] to fight with incoming enemy [UNIT]s : [INVADER] and [Invader Combat]
    > Get taken over and the [Control] transfered to [INVADER]'s player if it eliminated all [UNIT]s in its [UNIT CONTAINER] and still has [INVADER]s remaining

  - [UNIT] 
    > Has [HP]
    > [Movement] stats
    > [Crowd Physic] charateristics

    > Is spawn by ordering a [BASE] to [RALLY] [UNIT]s to another [BASE]
    > No direct control from player to individual [UNIT]s

    > All opposing [UNIT]s will fight when met each other in middle of their ways
      > [Melee Combat] collisions

    > Colliding ANY opposing [BASE] : enter it as [INVADER]
    > Colliding targetted ally [BASE] : enter and join into its [UNIT CONTAINER]


Challenges
  - Everyone starts the game with only a few [BASE]s already under [Control] and small amount of [UNIT]s in their [UNIT CONTAINER]s
    > initial game state limiting the options

  - There are neutral [BASE] not in any player's [Control], with some defenders inside, opposing and obstructing everyone else
    > serve as both a direct obstacle and also a tool to the game-ending condition

  - The level design and layout encourage tactical decisions
    > slowly introduce new mechanices, new [UNIT] types, freshing out scenarios and challenges
    > passive tutorial progression (and option to skip directly into skill-check)




=============================
[UNIT] Unit
=============================
Synonym: Troop, Soldier
=============================
- The main combatant of the game
  > one [UNIT] refering to a singular GameObject
  > Often formed and rallied in group / army

- The key element of the game.
- Active GameObeject, and LOTS of them!

- Many type of [UNIT] will have different role / propose, stats
  (and special mechanics unique to a type, in the future)

- Primary Features:
  > Movement
    > Destination
      > [BASE]
    > Pattern (mid-journey behaviour)
    > Speed

  > [Crowd Physic]

  > Basic stats
    > TeamID (player ownership)
    > HP
    
  > Melee combat stats
    > Damage
    > 'Knockback Force' (post-combat consequence)


- Interactions
  A> Collision with an opposing [UNIT] : [Melee Combat]

  B> Approaching [BASE]
    B0> Priority: ignore and be exempted from all [UNIT] interactions, only if [BASE] entry occured

    B1> Entering the [BASE] by condition :
      1.1> Enemy [BASE], regardless if it's the intended target
        1.1.1> Transfer data to target [BASE], to be resolved as [INVADER]

      1.2> Friendly [BASE], only if it is the assigned destination
        1.2.1> Transfer data to target [BASE], to be resolved as reinforcement

    B2> GameObject be removed* from the game

=============================
[BASE] Base
=============================
Synonym: Fort, Camp, Garrison
=============================
- Serve as a source of [UNIT] generation; manpower "Recruitment"
- Dynamically changing elements
  > with [UNIT] as the primary means, [RALLY] to and take over the [BASE]

- Also serve as key elements for game-ending condition
  > territoral control: acquire and gather up manpower, the 'resource' of the game
  > conquest: eliminate enemy's control over the [BASE]s, winning the game
  > defense: prevent enemy from doing the same: losing all controlled [BASE]s and you are defeated!

- Primary Features:
  > [UNIT] Recruitment
    > specific [UNIT] type
    > periodically, in rate of headcount per second

  > [UNIT] storage: [UNIT CONTAINER]
    > mini database representing types and numbers of [UNIT] the [BASE] currently holding

  > [UNIT] deployment: forming [ARMY] to [RALLY] to target [BASE]
    > Spawn [UNIT] GameObjects in a pattern / formation, assign and initialize stats and data

  > Data resolution with incoming friendly [UNIT]s
    > [wip] go straight to [UNIT CONTAINER] tally
    
  > Combat resolution against [INVADER] : [Invader Combat]

=============================
Crowd Physic
=============================
[UNIT] will not stacking / overlapping each other; repel from each other when squeezed / pressed; form cluster / formation
  > Simple and light-weight logic to handle crowd interactions
    > 'Size' (usually in radius of circle)
    > 'Weight' (resistance to knockback / crowding condition)
    > 'Repelling force' (for repelling other [UNIT] away)
    > Handle external forces, like "Knockback" from melee combats

[Obstacle] serve as static hitbox so [UNIT]s will not move through, but be nudged to go around it



=============================
Melee Combat
=============================
- one melee combat involves two opposing [UNIT]s collided into each other mid-way to their [RALLY] destination
- each [UNIT] attack the other [UNIT] with their melee stats: exactly one time each.
- Then, if survive, get knocked back (almost always).

- Singleton class to manage all the [Melee Combat]s to ensure that:
  > no duplicated resolutions, at least within the same frame / game tick
  > no overkills; no hitting the dead / dying [UNIT]s and suffer its counter-attack for free
  > special cases and logics
    1> [UNIT] will take [BASE] entry as a priority and ignore all other combats in the process



=============================
Invader Combat
=============================
- happen when a [UNIT] enters an opposing [BASE], creating [Invasion]
  > [BASE]-attacking [UNIT] is called [INVADER]
  > [BASE]-defending [UNIT] from its [UNIT CONTAINER] is called [DEFENDER]
  > Resolved by looping 1-1 [Melee Combat] until either (or both) [UNIT] is defeated
    > Iterate list of [INVADER]s against list of [UNIT CONTAINER]s



=============================
[UNIT CONTAINER] Unit Container
=============================
- database to store and represent [UNIT] a certain GameObject has stored within
- Raw data, in a list of [UNIT] types, each has
  > [UNIT STATS] object: to define [UNIT] type and its characteristics
  > headcount: total number of [UNIT]s something actually has
  > busycount: a way to reserve certain amount of [UNIT] for use with all sort of actions and mechanics
